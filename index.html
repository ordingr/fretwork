    How to drill it

    • Start at 60–72 BPM in triplets. Two clean passes before you raise the tempo, never sooner.
    • Say the degree out loud as you play it — the shape sticks faster when you know what note you are on.
    • Move dot labels to Degrees once the note names are automatic, then to None.
    • Shift without stopping: position 3 ascending straight into position 4 descending.
    • Space play/stop · ← → position · ↑ ↓ tempo
